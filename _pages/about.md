My research focuses on how **waves, turbulence, and kinetic instabilities** shape particle dynamics in space plasmas—.

I work on **theoretical plasma physica and kinetic/hybrid simulations**. A central theme in my research is understanding **wave–particle interactions** driven by non-thermal ion populations (e.g., **pickup ions**) and how these processes influence plasma fluctuations, transport, and observable signatures (such as ENA measurements).

**Current interests include:**
- Pickup-ion–driven instabilities and self-generated fluctuations in the outerheliosheath
- Multi-component ion distributions and their impact on turbulence and scattering  
- Kinetic hybrid simulations and analysis tools for wave–particle coupling  
- Connecting models and simulations to observations (e.g., IBEX/IMAP-relevant physics)

I’m always happy to connect about collaborations, simulations, or plasma wave physics—feel free to reach out by email.
